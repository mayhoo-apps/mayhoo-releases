# Mayhoo Dice release notes

Newest first. The app shows the notes for every version newer than the one you have.

## 0.2 (2026-11-28)
- Video Poker back to the original big layout; Hootzee two-part scorecard with box descriptions, die corners clipped; Puzzle # in the size dropdown; Tracks: darker grass, bold dots and X's
- Gin: Owl weighs near-melds less, throws loose high cards early; deadwood label clear of a raised card
- Gin: Owl discards by deadwood and the chance unseen cards help the rest of the hand
- Rummy: add regular Gin Rummy; Gin staging area label, deadwood label over the deadwood row
- Casino: Wise Owl advice in the owl box, like every other game
- Mayhoo Dice 0.1: Hootzee, Farkle, Shut the Box, Pig and Left Right Hoot; Hub lists it (GitHub builds only)

## 0.1 (2026-11-28)
- Mayhoo Dice 0.1: Hootzee, Farkle, Shut the Box, Pig and Left Right Hoot; Hub lists it (GitHub builds only)
- Oklahoma Gin: Owl keeps cards one away from a meld (pairs, suited neighbors)
- Fixes: Phase skip picker, DREW tag in Phase and Gin, Crazy Eights suit picker/draw animation/aces low, Video Poker any bet, Pyramid under-waste card in play
- Duel: add Snakes (two players or vs the Owl); catalog lists the five new games
- Casino: add Video Poker (Jacks or Better); menu: lone last tile same size
- Solitaire: add Pyramid
- Rummy: add Oklahoma Gin
- Cards: add Crazy Eights; tiles for the five new games
- Puzzles: Circuits is now connect-the-pairs (colored terminals, wires can't cross, fill every square)
- Puzzles: Circuits (turn wires so every bulb is in a closed loop from + to -; shorts light nothing; 5x5 to 8x8)
- Phase Rummy: after someone goes out, everyone plays until the dealer's turn (dealer plays last); show your own score
- Phase Rummy: the player who discards a skip chooses who loses a turn; bots skip whoever is closest to winning
